## 0.3.2

- **Modpacks are welcome.** You may include this mod, unmodified, in any modpack on any platform — including modpacks monetised through a platform's reward programme — without asking. Reuploading the mod as a standalone download, or reusing its assets in another project, is still not allowed.
- Repository and packaging metadata cleaned up for the first public release.

## Included from 0.3.1

- The **Warden** now drops the Echo Upgrade Smithing Template: 10% when killed by a player, +1 percentage point per Looting level. Non-player kills do not drop it, and the Warden keeps its vanilla sculk catalyst and XP drops.
- Bonus echo shards from sculk blocks now scale with the pickaxe's **Fortune** enchantment: 10% / 15% / 20% / 25% for Fortune 0/I/II/III. A hoe in your main hand with an upgraded pickaxe in your offhand reads only the pickaxe's Fortune, and the two never stack.

No new items, no NBT or network changes — existing worlds and pickaxes are unaffected.
